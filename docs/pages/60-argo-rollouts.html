{{ page_head("Roll out a new version in steps") }} {% call stage("understand") %} {{ section("idea", "The idea") }}

A Deployment replaces its pods by a rolling update, which runs to the end without stopping. A Rollout replaces them in steps: it moves a share of the pods to the new version, pauses, and goes on.

A workload moves from a Deployment to a Rollout by changing its kind and its strategy. The pod template stays as it is.
